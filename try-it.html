Salo Salo Sabotage · Free Browser Demo

Play a round right now. No cards required.

Collect Food, Family, and Fun before your tita shows up with the latest tsismis and takes it all down. Play solo against Tita AI, or pass the phone for two players.

Built for your phone — on desktop it plays the same, just in this frame.

1The goal

Collect three cards each of Food, Family, and Fun before anyone else does. Nine cards, three categories — get all nine and shout “Salo-Salo!”

2The sabotage

Everything that can go wrong at a family party comes back as a card — Tita’s Tea, Bagyo, Brownout, an Unexpected Guest. Play counters like Mano Po and Kapwa to block them.

3Two ways to play

Solo pits you against Tita AI for a quick round on your own. Pass & Play hands the phone back and forth between two players — hands stay hidden until it’s your turn.
