Two ways to make a dance

Narrative vs. abstract dance

Narrative

Tells a story with characters, a plot, and feelings. It has a beginning, middle, and end.

Story ballets · Vinta · Tinikling

Abstract

Explores shape, rhythm, energy, or music. There is no story to follow.

Many contemporary and hip hop pieces

Story ballets

Ballets tell stories through movement and mime

The Nutcracker

Clara's Christmas gift, a nutcracker, comes to life and takes her on a journey to the Land of Sweets.

Swan Lake

A princess is cursed to be a swan by day. A prince tries to break the spell.

Giselle

A village girl's heart is broken, and as a spirit she still protects the one she loved.

How they do it: characters, costumes, music that sets the mood, and mime, gestures that stand in for words.

Filipino folk dance

Filipino dances tell stories too

Zamboanga · Mindanao

Vinta

The vinta is a wooden boat with bright, striped sails. Dancers show it gliding and rocking on the sea, and some balance on bamboo poles held by others.

Leyte · Visayas

Tinikling

Dancers copy the tikling bird stepping quickly between bamboo poles, the way it dodged traps in the rice fields.

October is Filipino American History Month. These dances carry stories of everyday life, nature, and the sea.

Learn it · Vinta-inspired

The water moves

1. Waves

Arms out to the side, ripple from shoulder to fingertips. 8 counts.

2. Paddle

Both hands pull an oar on one side, then the other. 4 counts each side.

3. Sway

Feet in second, rock side to side like the boat on the sea. 8 counts.

4. Sail and glide

One arm up high as the mast, small relévé steps forward. 8 counts.
