Kuwento Quest

A story game where children and grown-ups learn Tagalog and Baybayin side by side. Each of you has a role, so you need each other to finish the story.

We're still building it. Join the list and we'll let you know when your family can play at home.

How it plays

Everyone plays. Nobody just watches.

Seer

Usually a younger child. Finds what's hidden in the pictures.

Scribe

Usually an older child. Decodes Lola's Baybayin pages and writes first.

Word Keeper

Usually a grown-up. Reads Lola's notebook out loud and learns the words right alongside the children.

This is an early preview. Artwork and audio are still in progress.
